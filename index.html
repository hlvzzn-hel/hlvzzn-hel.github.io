Project 01 · Quantitative Finance & AI

SmartInvest — 8-Stage Analytical & AI Case Study

Problem StatementBeginner and intermediate retail investors often make emotional stock decisions due to fragmented market indicators and complex portfolio math.
Objective & RoleProvide data-driven portfolio weights and trend signals. Individual role: project leadership, idea formulation, stock/IHSG trend modeling, AI Advisor & model integration.
Tools & MethodologiesPython, TensorFlow, BiLSTM + MultiHead Attention, MVEP, Single Index Model (SIM), CAPM, Yahoo Finance API, REST API.
Key Findings & ResultsAchieved >90% average classification accuracy on stock & IHSG trend evaluation tests, integrated with automated portfolio weight recommendations.
01. DATA COLLECTIONHistorical equity prices, trading volume, LQ45/IDX80, IHSG, and BI-Rate via Yahoo Finance.
02. DATA PREPROCESSINGMissing-value checks, temporal synchronization, validation, and price matrix transformation.
03. FEATURE ENGINEERINGLog returns, covariance matrix, Beta & Alpha, Sharpe Ratio, and CAPM expected return.
04. EXPLORATORY ANALYSISRisk-vs-return profiling, correlation heatmaps, and asset volatility analysis.
05. PORTFOLIO OPTIMIZATIONOptimal capital allocation weights calculated via MVEP, SIM, and CAPM.
06. AI TREND CLASSIFICATIONBiLSTM + MultiHead Attention classifying stock and IHSG market regimes.
07. RECOMMENDATION SYSTEMAdjusts portfolio recommendations based on predicted IHSG and stock trend regimes.
08. WEB DEPLOYMENTInteractive dashboard with parameter configuration and AI Advisor narrative output.
